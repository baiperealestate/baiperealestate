BEFORE YOU RENT

What Should You Understand First?

The rental process involves more than finding a property and agreeing on a monthly amount.

01

Rental Pricing

Rental value should be considered in relation to location, property condition, furnishing, amenities, comparable properties and current market demand.

02

Lease Terms

Before signing, both parties should understand the rental period, monthly rent, deposit, inclusions, responsibilities and other agreed terms.

03

Tenant Selection

Property owners may benefit from a structured screening process that considers identity, employment, income, intended use and other relevant information.

04

Property Presentation

Professional photography, accurate information and clear rental conditions help attract suitable tenants and create stronger first impressions.

05

Property Handover

A documented handover can help clarify the property's condition, keys, meter readings, inventory and responsibilities at the start of the rental.

06

Communication

Clear communication between owner and tenant can reduce misunderstandings and establish realistic expectations throughout the rental.

TAX CONSIDERATIONS

Rental Taxes in Curaçao

Tax treatment depends on the type and duration of the rental. Always verify your specific situation with the Curaçao Tax Department or a qualified tax professional.

01

Long-Term Residential Rental

According to the Curaçao Tax Department, rental of an immovable property that is furnished, intended or used for permanent residence and rented for at least one year is subject to 0% turnover tax.

02

Short-Term Accommodation

For accommodation rented to persons staying for a short period of up to 90 days, the applicable turnover tax rate is currently 7%.

03

Other Rental Periods

The Curaçao Tax Department states that rentals exceeding 90 days but shorter than one year are subject to a 6% turnover tax in the applicable circumstances.

04

Rental Income

Rental income may have income-tax consequences. The Curaçao Tax Department states that rental proceeds are taxable and should be included in the applicable income-tax return.

Important: Tax rules can depend on the property, rental structure, rental period and circumstances of the owner. This page is general information and is not tax or legal advice.

PROPERTY OWNERS

Understanding OZB

The Onroerendezaakbelasting (OZB) is a property tax in Curaçao. The official tax authority states that the tax is based on the value of the property and uses progressive rates.

0.4% First NAf. 350,000
0.5% Next NAf. 400,000
0.6% Amount above NAf. 750,000

OZB treatment can vary depending on the property and ownership circumstances. Verify your specific position with the Curaçao Tax Department.
