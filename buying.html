FINANCING

Resident or international buyer?

Residency can influence financing conditions. Your bank will determine your actual eligibility, loan amount, interest rate and terms.

01

Curaçao Residents

Residents may have access to financing structures based on local lending policies, income, age, credit profile and the property being purchased.

Indicative financing range Up to approximately 80–90% LTV*
02

Non-Residents

International buyers can purchase property in Curaçao. Financing requirements may be different and typically require a larger own contribution.

Indicative financing range Approximately 60–70% LTV*

*Indicative ranges only. Financing is determined solely by the lender and may vary based on residency, income, age, credit profile, source of funds, property value, property type and lender policies. Bai Pe Real Estate does not provide mortgage approval or financial advice.

Depending on the transaction, these can include transfer tax, notary costs, cadastre/registration costs, appraisal, inspection, financing-related expenses, insurance and other applicable costs.

BUYER INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners buy property in Curaçao?

Yes. Non-residents can purchase property in Curaçao. Financing, documentation and other requirements can differ depending on the buyer and transaction.

Do I need to live in Curaçao to buy property?

No. You do not have to be a resident of Curaçao to purchase property. However, residency can affect financing options and requirements.

Can I finance a property purchase?

Financing may be available depending on your financial profile, residency, income, property and lender requirements. Final financing decisions are made by the bank.

How much should I have available?

This depends on the purchase price, financing structure, residency and applicable transaction costs. You should plan for more than the down payment alone.

Should I get a property inspection?

A professional inspection can help identify potential issues with an existing property before you complete the purchase.

Can Bai Pe help if another agency has the property?

Where appropriate, we can work with other real estate professionals to help buyers access suitable property opportunities.

Who handles the legal transfer?

The notary handles the legal aspects of the property transfer and the applicable deed and registration process.

Can I speak with Bai Pe before I am ready to buy?

Yes. A conversation can help you understand the market, clarify your requirements and determine what preparation may be appropriate before beginning your search.
