WHY CURAÇAO

More than a destination. A place to live, invest and build a future.

Curaçao is a southern Caribbean island with a distinctive combination of Caribbean lifestyle, Dutch influence, international connectivity and a diverse local community.

For people considering a move, a second home, a rental property or a long-term investment, understanding the island is just as important as understanding the property itself.

This guide is designed to give you a professional starting point before you make a real estate decision in Curaçao.

CURAÇAO AT A GLANCE

Know the island first.

LOCATION Southern Caribbean

Curaçao is located approximately 12° north of the Equator and just north of Venezuela.

STATUS Country within the Kingdom of the Netherlands

Curaçao is an autonomous country within the Kingdom of the Netherlands.

CLIMATE Warm year-round

The island has a warm tropical climate with average temperatures around 27°C.

CURRENCY Caribbean Guilder — XCG

The Caribbean guilder became legal tender in Curaçao and Sint Maarten on March 31, 2025.

LIVING IN CURAÇAO

What is life really like here?

Life in Curaçao is shaped by its location, climate, culture and international character. The island combines a relaxed Caribbean lifestyle with Dutch institutional influence and a multicultural population.

Culture & language

Papiamentu is the island's principal local language. Dutch, Spanish and English are also widely spoken. This multilingual environment makes Curaçao accessible to people from different international backgrounds.

Willemstad & heritage

Willemstad is the capital of Curaçao and is known for its historic architecture and colorful districts. The historic area of Willemstad has been recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997.

Lifestyle

Curaçao offers beaches, water activities, dining, cultural events, nature, nightlife and outdoor recreation. The island's location outside the main Atlantic hurricane belt is another factor often considered by people choosing where to live or invest.

International accessibility

Curaçao is connected to international destinations in North America, South America, Europe and the Caribbean through air travel.

INVESTING IN CURAÇAO

Why consider Curaçao for real estate?

Real estate decisions should always be based on proper research, financial planning and professional advice. However, several characteristics make Curaçao an interesting market to investigate.

01

International appeal

Curaçao attracts residents, visitors and investors from different international markets. This creates opportunities across residential, rental and investment property segments.

02

Rental potential

Depending on location, property type, management, demand and applicable regulations, real estate may provide opportunities for long-term or short-term rental strategies.

03

Lifestyle-driven demand

The island's climate, beaches, culture and international character contribute to its appeal as a residential and second-home destination.

04

Caribbean location

Curaçao's position in the southern Caribbean, outside the main hurricane belt, is an important geographic characteristic for people evaluating Caribbean property markets.

05

Currency stability

The Caribbean guilder has a fixed exchange rate with the U.S. dollar. The official rate is Cg 1.79 per USD.

06

Multiple property strategies

Depending on your objectives, a property may be considered for personal use, long-term rental, short-term accommodation, resale or a combination of strategies.
